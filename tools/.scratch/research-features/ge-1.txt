What makes Chromebook Plus different from a standard Chromebook?

Chromebook Plus devices offer more power, speed, and memory than standard Chromebooks. For educators, this means smoother multitasking and exclusive AI-powered tools, such as enhanced video call features (like noise cancellation and lighting adjustment) and generative AI to help with writing and organizing lessons.

Compare Chromebook models
See Chromebook Plus for teachers

How do Chromebooks support students with different learning needs?

Accessibility is built in, not bolted on. Every Chromebook includes features like Reading Mode to reduce distractions, Select-to-speak for audio support, and a built-in screen magnifier. These tools allow students to customize their learning environment without needing additional software.

Explore all accessibility features
Download the Accessibility Guide (PDF)

Can students use Chromebooks if they don't have internet at home?

Yes. Students can use "Offline Mode" in Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides to continue working on their assignments without a Wi-Fi connection. Any changes made will automatically sync to their Google Drive once they are back online.

Are Chromebooks really safe from ransomware?

Yes. To date, there have been zero successful reported ransomware attacks on ChromeOS. Chromebooks have built-in security, including verified boot, data encryption, and sandboxing, which ensures that even if one tab is compromised, the rest of the system remains safe.

Learn more about security on ChromeOS

How long will a new fleet of Chromebooks receive updates?

Google is committed to long-term value. Starting in 2024, all Chromebooks released from 2021 onwards receive 10 years of automatic security and feature updates. This ensures your investment stays secure and functional for a decade.

Check the Auto Update policy for specific models

How does the ChromeOS Education Upgrade simplify management?

ChromeOS Education Upgrade unlocks the full potential of the Google Admin console. You can remotely manage thousands of devices, configure 500+ policies (like blocking sites or pre-installing apps), and even disable lost or stolen devices instantly from a single central dashboard.
