that's one heck of a lot of work, it may work as a fan creation but in my experience it takes about 20 people to reliably provide one hour of content per day and that's real world.

something so in-depth as far as role play goes would require a level of structure and scripting that even the biggest production company would find difficult to do live and challenging at best to deliver as recorded content.

for even a few hours of airtime a day you would require a volunteer staff of around 100 people and you would have to recruit new members so often that every show would have a help wanted advert.

i'm not saying it cant be done, but given the level of expenditure involved to do even a passable job at it i cannot ever see it happening.

no problems at all with your english 

the issue there would more than likely be licencing, as the station would be entirely game centric and money would change hands you would quickly fall foul of IP and copyright issues.

a quick look at the media usage rules is enough to convince you of that.

saying that a 3 hour a week podcast made by a fan team is doable but would still require a lot of work from the person who is organising it.

my advice would be that as a first step you post in fan creations asking for contributors and come up with some possible names/logos for the website that is sure to follow soon after.

possibly the most challenging part will be finding your place in the ED universe as we already have Lave radio for factual game discussion and news as well as a few role play/dramatisation based podcasts.

if you decide to go ahead with it good luck to you but you are going to be busy busy busy and not have a lot of time left to actually play the game.
