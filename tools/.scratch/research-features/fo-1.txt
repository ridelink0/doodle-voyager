Woakes said:
but has it ever been confirmed by the actual dev team?
The dev team communicate via the community managers, because programming software and talking to grumpy people are very distinct skill sets. It's all one company; you can assume that the community managers are making statements with some level of official backing unless they say otherwise.

They're highly unlikely to say "ship interiors will never happen" (and haven't yet). After all, in N years time they might decide to do them.
They're even less likely to say "ship interiors will happen" at any notice longer than 2-3 months before their planned release.



Of course, "ship interiors" covers a really wide range of potential features which go from "already implemented" (our ships have interiors and we can sit in chairs in them) to "you could play for years without exiting the ship or flying it anywhere". So even if they did rule out one person's interpretation of ship interiors that doesn't mean that they wouldn't implement someone else's interpretation the next week.
