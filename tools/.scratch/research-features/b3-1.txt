Requesting Joystick (HOTAS) support!
With recent updates to the game I can no longer play in VR (performance issues). So I thought I'd try playing on a normal flat screen using Keyboard and Mouse. Very quickly I find myself struggling to pilot my ship with the mouse. The mouse just isn't the right way to fly a ship. No problem though, I have a nice joystick and throttle here. I'll just set them up to the side, so whenever I jump in my ship I can use a real HOTAS setup to fly.

Except I can't. The game won't let me bind the joystick or throttle. Really? It won't accept any generic Windows input device? I found some people talking about workarounds to get your joystick to simulate an XBox controller. Ugh. Could we just get proper support for binding other input devices like joysticks? That would even make playing in VR easier.

Thanks.
