      DOODLE VOYAGER

      a ballpoint space shooter · walk your own ship across every charted galaxy

      on foot

      • WASD walk, Shift jog, Space hop
      • E use whatever you are looking at: the helm, the MP4 screen, the nav screen, the stove, the shower, the hull breach lever
      • V tapes and playlist, N next hold-music track
      • I storage, M map, K photo

      outside (after the breach)

      • WASD Space Ctrl jetpack · 120 s of air
      • E at the hole climbs back in and tapes it shut

      at the helm

      • mouse or arrow keys steer · hold right or Q to look around
      • W/S or the wheel throttle, A/D roll, X all stop
      • left or Space lasers (watch the heat) · Shift boost, burns more
      • L autopilot to the nearest pump that sells your fuel
      • C cruise drive: faster the emptier the space around you
      • T autopilot to the map target, then get up and walk
      • J warp: anywhere in under a minute, eats the tank
      • F dock at a pump or a Bazaar shop

      drone

      • G launch and recall · left or B drops a bomb
      • the bay prints a new bomb every 20 s while the drone is home

      Needs a keyboard and a mouse.
